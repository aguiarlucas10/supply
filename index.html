This guide walks you through the full quoting and ordering flow — from initial demand through payment organization.

01
Define the Demand

Before anything, map out the products needed for the cycle. Go to Products and ensure all items are registered in the catalog with SKU, Main Category and Sub Category. You can import in bulk via CSV.

02
Create a Process

Go to Processes → New Process. A process represents one quoting round (e.g. "Summer 2025"). Give it a clear name and set status to Open.

03
Add Products to the Process

Inside the process, click + Add Product or ↑ Import CSV to add the items for this round. At this stage, fill in process-specific details: quantity, plating, tool type, and whether you have a sample.

⚠️ Before confirming quantities, cross-check plating differences — a price significantly lower than normal may indicate a different bath (e.g. 1mic vs 3mic). Also confirm which suppliers hold samples of each product.
04
Invite Suppliers

Click + Invite Supplier inside the process. Choose the supplier and set a unique access password for this process. The supplier will use this password to access the portal.

Share the portal link and password with each supplier. The portal URL is the same as this app's URL.

05
Suppliers Respond

Each supplier logs into the portal with their password and sees only their assigned products. They can Accept, Reject, or submit a Counter-Proposal with their price, lead time, and notes.

06
Consolidate & Allocate

Go to Quotes and open the process. The Quote Matrix shows all supplier responses side by side per product. Identify the best prices and allocate quantities accordingly.

💡 Use the Edit button on each quote cell to manually adjust status or price if needed after a phone/email negotiation.
07
Confirm Orders with Suppliers

Once allocation is final, confirm each order with the respective supplier. Use the standard message template:

"Hi [name]! We reviewed and finalized the [product] order. Could you please confirm receipt and the production lead time of 60 days. Once everything is confirmed, we will organize the payments right away. Thank you!"
08
Organize Payments

After all suppliers confirm, organize the deposit payments (entrada) for each order. Mark the process as Finalized once payments are arranged.
